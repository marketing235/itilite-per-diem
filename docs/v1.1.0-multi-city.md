## 1. Scope

**In v1.1.0**

1. **Multi-city trips:** one trip, up to 5 destinations (NYC 2 nights, then Pittsburgh 2 nights).
2. **Same-day trips:** an "away more than 12 hours" switch, and more than one city allowed on a day trip.
3. **Small fixes** that ride on the calculator markup change (section 11).
4. **Copy updates** in Webflow that ship with the feature (section 12).

**Not in v1.1.0**

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Separate, unrelated trips ("Add trip") | Decided: multi-city is the model. Separate trips = run the calculator once per trip. |
| Meal deductions in the calculator | v1.2.0. Needs per-day meal inputs, GSA's meal breakdown table and the first/last-day deduction rule. |
| Times of day | Federal rules decide by calendar date and where you sleep; time only matters for day trips, covered by the 12-hour switch. |
| Tax note for day trips | Pending approval from finance (Vishal). |
| Alaska, Hawaii, international | Unchanged: out of scope. |
| Saving or sharing trips | Not requested. |

## 3. The federal rules this implements

Source: Federal Travel Regulation, 41 CFR part 301-11 (current text, checked 15 Oct 2026).

| Rule | FTR |
|---|---|
| Per diem only when in travel status for **more than 12 hours** | §301-11.1(c) |
| Trip of 24 hours or more: **75%** of the M&IE rate on the day of departure and the last day; full days 100% | §301-11.20(a) |
| No lodging, more than 12 but less than 24 hours: **75%** of the M&IE rate for each calendar day | §301-11.20(a) |
| Days that require lodging: M&IE rate of the **TDY location or stopover point** (where you stay) | §301-11.102 |
| No lodging, 12–24 hours: rate of the TDY site, **"or the highest M&IE rate applicable when multiple locations are involved"** | §301-11.102 |
| Travelling to a new TDY site: rate of the **new site** (where you are at midnight) | §301-11.102 |
| Returning to your official station: **"the M&IE rate applicable to the previous day of travel"** | §301-11.102 |
| Lodging: actual cost up to the lodging rate of the place you stay | §301-11.100 (checked in the 2011 edition) |

Plain-language summary for the page and the team: *each night and each day use the rate of the city
where you sleep that night; the day you go home uses the previous night's city; only the first and
last day of the whole trip pay 75%.*

## 4. Trip model

```
trip = {
  depart:  'YYYY-MM-DD',            // existing Departure date field
  return:  'YYYY-MM-DD',            // existing Return date field
  over12:  true | false,            // only read when depart === return
  destinations: [                   // 1 to 5, in travel order
    { state: 'NY', location: '266' | 'std', arrive: null },          // destination 1: arrives on depart
    { state: 'PA', location: '318',          arrive: '2026-10-14' }  // destination k >= 2
  ]
}
```

- **Destination 1** has no date field: it starts on the departure date (it uses the existing State
  and City or county fields).
- **Destination k (k ≥ 2)** has an **Arrive on** date: the day the traveller moves there.
- **Nights at destination k** run from its arrive date to the night before the next destination's
  arrive date (for the last destination: the night before the return date).
- **Overnight trip** (return > depart) is valid only when
  `depart < arrive₂ < arrive₃ < … < arriveₙ < return`, i.e. every destination has at least one night.
- **Same-day trip** (return = depart): Arrive on fields are hidden and ignored; 1 to 5 destinations
  allowed (places visited that day).

## 5. Calculation rules (v1.1)

Rules 1–8 of the phase-4 spec still apply (UTC dates, fiscal year per date, `did` fallback to that
year's standard, DC has no standard, money keeps cents, 365-day limit, dates outside `meta.json`).
Changes and additions:

**Overnight trips**

1. **Night n** (each date from depart to return − 1) belongs to destination k where
   `arrive_k ≤ n < arrive_(k+1)`. Lodging = that destination's `lodging[month of n]` for FY(n).
2. **M&IE for day d:**
   - d < return: the destination of **night d** (where they sleep that night). This covers the
     first day and every changeover day.
   - d = return: the destination of **night return − 1** (the previous day's rate).
3. **75%** applies to the departure day and the return day **of the whole trip** only. Changeover
   days are full days.
4. Incidentals ($5 full day, $3.75 on 75% days) and meals (M&IE − incidentals) are unchanged.
5. Each destination resolves its own rate per fiscal year with the existing fallback. If its `did`
   is missing in a year and the state's `standard` is `null` (DC), error:
   "Rates for {place} aren't available for all of your dates."

**Same-day trips**

6. Switch **off**: lodging, meals, incidentals and total are $0. Message:
   "Day trips of 12 hours or less don't qualify for per diem under GSA rules." Export stays disabled.
7. Switch **on**: M&IE = 75% × the **highest** M&IE rate among the destinations for FY(depart)
   (tie: the earliest destination). Incidentals $3.75, meals = M&IE − $3.75, lodging $0.
   Record which destination's rate was used (`rateUsed`).
8. One destination with the switch on gives exactly the v1.0.1 result (golden test 3: $55.50).

**Validation order** (first failing check wins, following the form top to bottom; messages in
section 6): trip dates (phase-4 rule 8) → destination 1 state, then city or county → each further
destination in order: Arrive on present, inside the trip, not before the previous destination, then
state, then city or county → finally, every destination has at least one night. Same-day trips skip
the Arrive on checks.

**Code shape (suggested, not locked):**
`calculateTrip({ departIso, returnIso, over12, meta, files: { [fy]: { [ST]: data } }, destinations })`
returns `{ ok, days, nights, lodging, mie, meals, incidentals, total, rateUsed, rows[], destinations[] }`.
Each row adds `destination` (index) and `place`. Keep `calculate()` from v1.0 as a thin wrapper
(one destination) so the 9 existing golden tests run unchanged. Expose `calculateTrip` on
`window.ItilitePerDiem` for debugging.

## 6. Form behaviour

**Default state (one destination):** identical to v1.0.1.

**Adding destinations**

- An **"Add another destination"** button sits under the fields.
- Each click inserts a destination row: legend "Destination k", **Arrive on** date, **State**,
  **City or county**, and a **Remove** button.
- The new row's State is **prefilled with the previous destination's state** (most multi-city trips
  stay in one state; easy to change). Its City or county must still be chosen.
- With 2 or more destinations, a "Destination 1" title appears above destination 1's State field.
- At 5 destinations the button is disabled and the note "Up to 5 destinations per trip." shows.
- **Remove** deletes the row; later rows renumber (legend, labels, ids). Destination 1 can't be removed.
- Arrive on inputs get `min` = previous destination's arrive date + 1 day (depart + 1 for
  destination 2) and `max` = return − 1 day. Validation still runs (typed dates bypass min/max).

**Same-day trips**

- When both dates are set and equal, the switch **"I'm away for more than 12 hours"** appears
  (checked by default) and every Arrive on field hides. Its value is kept while the page is open.
- When the dates differ again, the switch hides and the Arrive on fields reappear with their
  previous values.

**Messages** (exact text; `{place}` = the chosen city, or "destination k" if none yet)

| Situation | Message |
|---|---|
| Row k has no state | Choose a state for destination {k}. |
| Row k has no city or county | Choose a city or county for destination {k}. |
| Row k has no Arrive on date | Choose the date you arrive at destination {k}. |
| Arrive on outside the trip | Destination {k}'s date must be between your departure and return dates. |
| Arrive on earlier than the previous one | Destination {k} starts before destination {k−1}. Check the dates. |
| A destination has no night | No night in {place}? Remove it: per diem follows where you sleep each night. |
| Day trip, switch off | Day trips of 12 hours or less don't qualify for per diem under GSA rules. |

Destination 1 keeps the v1.0.1 messages ("Choose a state", "Choose a city or county").

**Summary line** (after a valid result)

| Trip | Example |
|---|---|
| One destination (unchanged) | 3 days in San Francisco, 1–3 Oct 2026. Total allowance $788. |
| Two destinations | 5 days in New York City and Pittsburgh, 12–16 Oct 2026. Total allowance $1,385. |
| Three or more | 6 days in New York City, Albany and Boston / Cambridge, 9–14 Nov 2026. Total allowance $1,775. |
| Same day, several cities | 1 day in Albany and New York City, 12 Oct 2026. New York City's rate applies (the highest). Total allowance $69. |

Standard-rate destinations use "{State} (standard rate)", as in v1.0.1.

**Unchanged:** automatic recalculation, the five result boxes, lazy loading (IntersectionObserver
400px or first focus), one fetch per file per page view, `data-state` preselects destination 1 on
state pages, changing a state clears only that destination's location.
Location menus are built per destination from the fiscal year of **its** start date (destination 1:
departure date; others: Arrive on date, else departure date, else today), rebuilt when that fiscal
year changes, keeping the selection when the same `did` exists.

- Header lines: unchanged, except the location line.
  - One destination: unchanged (`San Francisco, California` / `Standard rate, California`).

- BOM, CRLF, quoting, formula guard and plain numbers: unchanged.
- Same-day trip with the switch off: Export stays disabled.
