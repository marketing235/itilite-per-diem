# v1.3.0: Compare cities (specification)

> Save as `docs/v1.3.0-compare-cities.md` in the repo. Builds on `docs/v1.2.0-separate-trips.md`,
> `docs/v1.1.0-multi-city.md` and `docs/phase-4-calculator.md`, which stay locked. Everything below is
> **locked** unless marked *(verify)*; propose before changing anything else. Decided 22 Oct 2026
> with the team. Meal deductions move from v1.3.0 to **v1.4.0**.

## 1. Scope

v1.2.0 adds up trips. v1.3.0 adds a second mode, **Compare cities**: one set of dates, 2 to 5 cities,
each city's allowance side by side, the lowest one highlighted. Nothing is added up.

Why: people choosing where to hold an offsite, comparing assignments, or budgeting before the
destination is set. Today they can only change the city and remember the number, or try two trips
on the same dates and hit the overlap error.

**Decisions (22 Oct 2026):**
1. Up to **5 cities**, at least 2. Every city uses the **same dates**. One city per option (no
   multi-city inside a comparison).
2. The **lowest total is highlighted** ("Lowest" badge). Ties at the lowest: every tied city gets it.
   If all cities are equal, or all are $0, nothing is highlighted.
3. **Day trips allowed**, with **one 12-hour switch** for all cities (same rule as Calculate). Switch
   off: every city is $0, the 12-hour notice shows, Export stays off.
4. The same city twice is an error.
5. Calculate mode (v1.2.0) is unchanged.

**Not in v1.3.0:** meal deductions (v1.4.0), comparing multi-city trips, different dates per city,
saving comparisons.

## 2. Model

```
comparison = { departIso, returnIso, over12, cities: [ city, city, ... ] }   // 2 to 5, in the order entered
city = { state, location, stateName }                                         // one v1.1 destination
```

Each city is calculated as a one-destination v1.1 trip with the shared dates and switch, using the
unchanged `calculateTrip`. So every city gets its own 75% first and last day, fiscal-year handling,
standard-rate fallback and rate-gap checks, exactly as a single trip.

## 3. Calculation and validation rules

- New in `calc-core.js`: `MAX_CITIES = 5`, `calculateCompare(comparison)` and `compareNeeded(comparison, meta)`
  (the rate files to load, like `planNeeded`). `calculateTrip`, `calculate` and `calculatePlan` stay
  byte-for-byte unchanged.
- **Order of checks**, first failure wins:
  1. Dates, with the existing v1.1 messages, unprefixed (`field`: `depart` or `return`).
  2. Each city in order: State chosen, then City chosen, with the v1.1 prompts prefixed `City {n}: `
     (prompts are not errors, as in v1.1).
  3. Duplicates: the later city is marked: "City {n} is the same as city {m}. Choose a different city."
     (`city` n-1, `field` `location`).
  4. Fewer than 2 complete cities: prompt "Add another city to compare." (not an error).
  5. Each city's own calculation (rate gaps, dates not published): its message prefixed `City {n}: `.
- **Result:** `{ ok, days, tripType, cities: [per-city calculateTrip result], lowest: [indexes], highest: [indexes] }`.
  `lowest` is empty when all totals are equal or all are 0. Money compared in cents, no rounding.
- Every error carries `city` (0-based) and `field`, like v1.2's `trip`.

## 4. Form behaviour

- A **mode switch** above the form: two buttons, **Calculate** and **Compare cities**, with
  `aria-pressed`. Calculate is the default on page load.
- Switching hides one panel and shows the other. Each mode keeps its own inputs.
- **First switch to Compare:** copy Calculate's trip 1 dates, switch value and destination 1 (state
  and city) into Compare's dates and city 1, when they are filled. City 2 starts empty.
- On **state pages**, city 1's State starts with the page's state (`data-state`), as in Calculate.
- Compare always shows at least **2 city rows**. Remove buttons are hidden while only 2 remain.
  **Add another city** adds a row (its State prefilled from the previous city), up to 5, then
  disables and shows "Up to 5 cities.".
- Focus: after Add another city, to the new row's State; after Remove, to Add another city; after a
  mode switch, focus stays on the switch button.
- Errors use the v1.1 pattern: the row's status line turns red with the message, the field gets
  `aria-invalid`, and the bottom message repeats it.
- Ids: compare dates `pd-cmp-depart`, `pd-cmp-return`, `pd-cmp-over12`; city rows
  `pd-city-{n}-state`, `pd-city-{n}-location`, `pd-city-{n}-status`, renumbered on remove like v1.2.

## 5. Results (Compare mode)

- The result panel hides the five stat boxes and the breakdown, and shows a **list, one line per
  city in entry order**: city name (v1.1 naming, e.g. "North Dakota (standard rate)"), a detail line
  "Lodging $226 · M&IE $170", and the total on the right.
- Lowest city or cities: class `is-lowest` and a visible "Lowest" badge.
- **Summary line** (bottom message):
  - 3 or more cities: "3 cities, 12–14 Oct 2026 (3 days). Lowest: North Dakota (standard rate) at $396. Highest: New York City at $946."
  - 2 cities: "2 cities, 12–14 Oct 2026 (3 days). Lowest: North Dakota (standard rate) at $396, $392 less than San Francisco."
  - Tie at the lowest: "Lowest: Wisconsin (standard rate) and North Dakota (standard rate) at $396."
  - All equal: "All cities have the same allowance: $69."
  - Day trip, switch off: the existing 12-hour notice.
- Export is enabled only while the comparison is valid and at least one city is above $0.
- The result-panel note (75% rule) stays visible in both modes.

## 6. Hook contract v1.3 (additions; all earlier hooks unchanged)

| Hook | Element | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `[data-pd="mode"]` | `<div role="group" hidden>` | Mode switch wrapper |
| `[data-pd-mode="calculate"]`, `[data-pd-mode="compare"]` | `<button aria-pressed>` | The two modes |
| `[data-pd="calc-panel"]` | `<div>` | Wraps the existing Calculate fields (from the first `.perdiem-calc_fields` through `add-trip-wrap`) |
| `[data-pd="compare-panel"]` | `<div hidden>` | The Compare fields |
| `[data-pd="cmp-depart"]`, `cmp-return`, `cmp-over12-wrap`, `cmp-over12` | as in Calculate | Shared dates and switch |
| `[data-pd="cmp-list"]` + `<template data-pd="cmp-template">` | `<div>`, `<template>` | City rows (`data-pd-city` = `title`, `remove`, `status`, `state`, `location`) |
| `[data-pd="cmp-add-wrap"]`, `cmp-add`, `cmp-add-note` | as `add-wrap` | Add another city, "Up to 5 cities." |
| `[data-pd="result-calc"]` | `<div>` | Wraps the existing stat boxes and breakdown |
| `[data-pd="result-compare"]` | `<div hidden>` | Compare results |
| `[data-pd="cmp-results"]` + `<template data-pd="cmp-result-template">` | `<ol>`, `<template>` | One line per city (`data-pd-item` = `place`, `dates`, `amount`, `badge`) |

**Backward compatibility:** if `mode`, `compare-panel` or `result-compare` is missing, Compare is
off and the script behaves exactly as v1.2.0. New markup with an older script: the mode switch and
the compare panel carry `hidden` and stay hidden; the two wrappers change nothing.

## 7. Export (CSV)

- Filename: `per-diem-compare-{depart}-to-{return}.csv`.
- Header lines: `ITILITE per diem comparison`, `Travel dates: 12–14 Oct 2026 (3 days)`,
  `Cities: 3`, then `Source: …`, `Generated {date}`, a blank line.
- Columns: `City, State, Lodging (USD), Meals (USD), Incidentals (USD), Total (USD), Lowest`.
  One row per city in entry order; `Lowest` is `Yes` or empty. No daily rows and no total row.
- BOM, CRLF, quoting, formula guard and plain numbers: unchanged.

## 8. Analytics (GA4 `G-K26RX2PTFK`)

- New event **`perdiem_compare`**, fired like `perdiem_calculate` (1.5 s after inputs settle, once per
  new valid comparison). Params: `state` (city 1), `cities` (2 to 5), `days`, `trip_type`
  (`overnight` | `day` | `day_short`), `location_type` across the cities.
- `perdiem_export` gets a new param **`mode`**: `calculate` or `compare` (compare exports carry the
  compare params).
- Never send dates, city names or anything typed.
- **GA4 admin after release:** register `cities` and `mode` as event-scoped custom dimensions.

## 9. Accessibility

- The mode switch is two real buttons with `aria-pressed`; the group has an accessible name.
- Each city row is a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` ("City 2").
- The "Lowest" badge is text, never colour alone.
- One live region (the bottom message), as before. Keyboard-only use must work end to end.
